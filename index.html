Prepare for your Yatharth Hospitals visit.

Preparing medical records and questions ahead of time can make your hospital visit more organized. Follow the instructions shared by your doctor or hospital department.

  • Carry previous prescriptions and relevant reports
  • Keep a current list of medicines and allergies
  • Note your symptoms and questions in advance
  • Confirm appointment time, location and instructions
